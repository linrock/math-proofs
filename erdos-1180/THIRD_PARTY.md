# Attribution and third-party sources

This development formalizes results on Erdős Problem #1180 (Shparlinski 2002, Glibichuk 2006), including `erdos_1180`, Glibichuk's $(8 + o(1)) \varepsilon^{-2}$ and $32 \varepsilon^{-2}$ large-prime bounds (`erdos_1180.variants.glibichuk`), the asymmetric $(k, k + 1)$ and $(1, k)$ prime-block staircase bounds $C_{\mathrm{asymp}}(\varepsilon) \le 8 k (k + 1)$ and $C_{\mathrm{asymp}}(\varepsilon) \le 8 k$ (giving $C_{\mathrm{asymp}}(\varepsilon) \le 16$ for $\varepsilon > 2/3$), Shparlinski's $O(\varepsilon^{-3})$ bound (`erdos_1180.variants.shparlinski`), both the all-primes (`K * ε⁻¹ ≤ C ε`) and thresholded large-prime pigeonhole formulations of `erdos_1180.variants.lower_bound` (together with the sharp lower bounds $\varepsilon^{-1} \le \lceil \varepsilon^{-1} \rceil_+ \le C_{\mathrm{asymp}}(\varepsilon)$ and the small-prime exponential bound $2^{\varepsilon^{-1}} \le 4 C(\varepsilon)$), the strict separation $C_{\mathrm{asymp}}(n^{-1}) < C(n^{-1})$ for $n \ge 15$, and the disproof of the unthresholded all-primes optimality statement (`erdos_1180.variants.optimal`).
