# Attribution and third-party sources

This package formalizes the Borwein–Loring construction ([*Some questions of Erdős and Graham on numbers of the form $\sum g_n / 2^{g_n}$*](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1990-0993927-5), *Math. Comp.* 54 (1990), 377–394), the extremal upper bound, structural divisibility and uniqueness theorems, punctured-interval family, and $n \le 10000$ verification of Szabolcs Tengely, Maciej Ulas, and Jakub Zygadło ([*On a Diophantine equation of Erdős and Graham*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.01501), *J. Number Theory* 214 (2020), 445–459, `arXiv:2008.01501`), and the $2^m$ infinite-representation construction by toggling $m$ disjoint Borwein–Loring blocks.

Locally authored proof modules in `Proofs/`, `Statement.lean`, `Challenge.lean`, `Solution.lean`, and `AxiomAudit.lean` are Copyright 2026 Linmiao Xu and licensed under the included `LICENSE` (Apache-2.0).
