This package formalizes the Formal Conjectures small-case classifications at `n = 3, 4, 6, 7` for [Erdős Problem 91](https://www.erdosproblems.com/91), posed by Paul Erdős.

Locally authored proof modules in `Proofs/`, `Statement.lean`, `Challenge.lean`, `Solution.lean`, and `AxiomAudit.lean` are Copyright 2026 Linmiao Xu and licensed under the included [LICENSE](LICENSE) (Apache-2.0).

The `distanceSet`, `distinctDistances`, `minimalDistinctDistances`, `IsOptimal`, `DilationEquivSimilar`, `equiTriangle`, and `UniqueMinimizer` definitions and the four linked theorem statements are adapted from [FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/91.lean](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/main/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/91.lean) from [google-deepmind/formal-conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures) under the [Apache-2.0 License](LICENSE), with original copyright notices preserved. The upstream unproved theorem file is not imported.
