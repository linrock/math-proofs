Locally authored and adapted modules in `Proofs/`, `Statement.lean`, `Challenge.lean`, `Solution.lean`, and `AxiomAudit.lean` are Copyright 2025 The Formal Conjectures Authors and 2026 Linmiao Xu, and are licensed under the included `LICENSE` (Apache-2.0).

`LocallyDense`, `H`, `c`, and the off-diagonal Ramsey recurrence (`HasRamseyProperty`) are adapted from `google-deepmind/formal-conjectures` (`FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/667.lean` and `FormalConjecturesUtil`) under the [Apache-2.0 License](third_party/FORMAL-CONJECTURES-LICENSE.txt). The adapted problem definitions were introduced by hbakkaoui-hash in Formal Conjectures PR #4370.
