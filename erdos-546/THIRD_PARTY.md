# Attribution and third-party sources

This package formalizes Benny Sudakov's affirmative resolution of Erdős problem #546 (*Advances in Mathematics* 227 (2011), 601–609; arXiv:1002.0095) and the finite bounded-degree embedding and hereditary sparse-cut ingredients cited from Graham, Rödl, and Ruciński (2000) and Fox and Sudakov (2009). No theorem from any paper is assumed as an axiom.

Locally authored proof modules in `Proofs/`, `Statement.lean`, `Solution.lean`, and `AxiomAudit.lean` are licensed under the included `LICENSE` (MIT).

The graph Ramsey definitions (`SimpleGraph.graphRamsey`, `SimpleGraph.diagonalGraphRamsey`) and problem statement (`Erdos546.erdos_546` / `erdos_546_original_statement`) in `Challenge.lean`, `Statement.lean`, `Solution.lean`, `Proofs/RamseyBasics546.lean`, and `Proofs/OriginalStatement546.lean` are adapted from [FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/546.lean](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/6fbb54f24ccc2e64dcfaffc28c58950e377110d2/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/546.lean) and [FormalConjecturesForMathlib/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Ramsey.lean](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/6fbb54f24ccc2e64dcfaffc28c58950e377110d2/FormalConjecturesForMathlib/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Ramsey.lean) from [google-deepmind/formal-conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures) under the [Apache-2.0 License](third_party/FORMAL-CONJECTURES-LICENSE.txt), with original copyright notices preserved. The upstream unproved theorem file is not imported.
