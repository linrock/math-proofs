# Attribution and third-party sources

This package formalizes results on Erdős Problem #33, including the two solved upstream variants `erdos_33.variants.one_mem_lowerBounds` and `erdos_33.variants.vanDoorn`, finite-resolution instances of Cilleruelo's step-function method yielding the rational bounds $5/4 = 1.25$, $63/50 = 1.26$, and $127/100 = 1.27$, and van Doorn's Fibonacci block construction. The exact infimum in the main open value question `erdos_33` remains open.

Locally authored proof modules in `Proofs/`, `Statement.lean`, `Challenge.lean`, `Solution.lean`, and `AxiomAudit.lean` are Copyright 2025–2026 The Formal Conjectures Authors, together with Copyright 2026 Linmiao Xu, and are licensed under the included `LICENSE` (Apache-2.0).

The weighted-representation lower bound and the Moser–Abel identity in `Proofs/SquareComplementBounds33.lean` follow Leo Moser, *On the additive completion of sets of integers*, Proc. Symposia in Pure Mathematics 8 (1965), 175–180. The step-function prefix-sum method follows Javier Cilleruelo, *The additive completion of k-th powers*, J. Number Theory 44(3) (1993), 237–243. The problem itself is posed in Paul Erdős, *Problems and results in additive number theory*, Colloque sur la Théorie des Nombres, Bruxelles (1955), Centre Belge de Recherches Mathématiques (1956), 127–137, with Problem 33 at p. 134. The asymptotic lower bound $4/\pi$ for additive complements of the squares, which is the best known result of this kind and is stronger than every rational bound proved here, was obtained independently by Cilleruelo, by Laurent Habsieger, *On the additive completion of polynomial sets*, J. Number Theory 51 (1995), 130–135, and by R. Balasubramanian and D. S. Ramana, *Additive complements of the squares*, C. R. Math. Acad. Sci. Soc. R. Can. 23 (2001), 6–11; those two papers are cited as background and are not formalized in this package. The shifted-block construction with Fibonacci widths is due to Wouter van Doorn, communicated in the discussion on erdosproblems.com/33.

The `AdditiveBasisCondition` definition and variant statement signatures in `Challenge.lean`, `Statement.lean`, and `Solution.lean` are adapted from [`FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/33.lean`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/e750f777b32aeae464c01809e0d223dd62e99fab/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/33.lean) under the [Apache-2.0 License](third_party/FORMAL-CONJECTURES-LICENSE.txt).
