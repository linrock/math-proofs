# Attribution and third-party sources

This package formalizes the negative resolution of Erdős problem #575 in both its literal unrestricted formulation (following the classic bipartite forest counterexample $\{K_{1,2}, 2K_2\}$ recorded in Yuval Wigderson's note [*The Erdős–Simonovits Compactness Conjecture Needs More Assumptions*](https://ywigderson.math.ethz.ch/math/static/Compactness.pdf)) and its substantive cycle-containing formulation (transporting the all-bipartite cyclic counterexample family from OpenAI's [*Ten advances in mathematics and theoretical computer science*, Chapter 10](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/ten-proofs-oai.pdf)).

Locally authored proof modules in [Proofs/Erdos575.lean](Proofs/Erdos575.lean), [Proofs/ForestCounterexample575.lean](Proofs/ForestCounterexample575.lean), [Statement.lean](Statement.lean), [Solution.lean](Solution.lean), and [AxiomAudit.lean](AxiomAudit.lean) are licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

- [Proofs/CompactnessAndDegeneracy.lean](Proofs/CompactnessAndDegeneracy.lean) is the Lean `v4.35.0-rc2` port (a three-line Mathlib API update to `SimpleGraph.mem_incidenceFinset` and `Finset.prod_le_prod₀`) of [`CompactnessAndDegeneracy.lean`](https://github.com/openai/ten-proofs/blob/94bc0feb6a9ff12c7d31d6de640a725c9d43d2b6/CompactnessAndDegeneracy.lean) from [`openai/ten-proofs`](https://github.com/openai/ten-proofs) at commit `94bc0feb6a9ff12c7d31d6de640a725c9d43d2b6`, distributed under the [Apache-2.0 License](third_party/OPENAI-TEN-PROOFS-LICENSE.txt).
- The problem statement structure and foundational definitions (`FiniteGraph`, `FamilyFree`, `familyExtremal`, `IsCyclicFamily`, `IsCompactFamily`) in [Challenge.lean](Challenge.lean), [Statement.lean](Statement.lean), and [Solution.lean](Solution.lean) follow [`google-deepmind/formal-conjectures`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures) ([issue #804](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/issues/804) and `FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/180.lean`), distributed under the [Apache-2.0 License](third_party/FORMAL-CONJECTURES-LICENSE.txt), with original copyright notices preserved.
