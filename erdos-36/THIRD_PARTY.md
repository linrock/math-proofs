# Attribution and third-party sources

This package formalizes results on Erdős Problem #36 (Erdős 1955, Scherk 1955, Świerczkowski 1958, Moser 1959), including Fekete limit convergence, classical lower and upper bounds, the combined Moser–Scherk inequality, and exact values up to $M(15) = 6$.

Locally authored proof modules in `Proofs/`, `Statement.lean`, `Challenge.lean`, `Solution.lean`, and `AxiomAudit.lean` are Copyright 2026 Linmiao Xu and licensed under the included `LICENSE` (Apache-2.0).
